# Emergency Plumbing SMB Leads Summary

This export contains only records that had enough public-source evidence to satisfy the strict filters without fabricating missing values. Markets were searched in the requested order, and cities with no high-confidence complete records were skipped.

## Included Lead Count

- Total qualified leads exported: 5
- Output file: `research/data/emergency_plumbing_smb_leads.csv`

## Qualification Notes

- Included industries match plumbing installation/repair, sewer/drain service, emergency plumbing, or plumbing contractor activity aligned with SIC 1711 and NAICS 238220.
- Included employee counts are between 2 and 15.
- Included estimated annual revenue values are between $200,000 and $1,000,000.
- Included contacts are owner-level decision makers.
- Included rows have a public phone number, business email address, and active website listed in public sources.
- Franchise and national-chain records were excluded.
- Records with missing websites, missing emails, employee counts outside the requested range, revenue outside the requested range, or only directory-only/generic unqualified evidence were excluded.
